METAL GEAR REX

Metal Gear REX is a bipedal nuclear-equipped tank developed by the US government under the codename "Project: Metal Gear."

It is capable of launching a nuclear strike from any location on Earth without the need for a missile silo or launch pad. Its railgun technology allows it to fire a stealth nuclear warhead at intercontinental ranges, making it the ultimate weapon of mass destruction.
